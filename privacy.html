Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

What BaseDrop stores: your Airtable personal access token, your license key and a random device ID, saved only in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local).

Where data goes: the token is sent only to api.airtable.com. Attachment files are downloaded directly from Airtable's file servers (*.airtableusercontent.com, dl.airtable.com) to your computer. BaseDrop has no server that receives your records or files.

License check: when you activate Pro, your license key and random device ID are sent to our license server, which asks Payhip whether the key is valid. We store the key and up to 3 device IDs to enforce the device limit. Nothing else is collected.

No analytics, no ads, no selling of data.

Not affiliated with Airtable.
